Important Notice – Test Phase 1

RailQuick is currently running in Test Phase 1. You may be able to place demo or test orders on our platform, but products will NOT be delivered to your seat at this stage.

This phase is only for testing, learning, and improving user experience. Seat delivery will be available very soon once we officially launch.

Information We Collect

During the test phase, we may collect basic information such as:

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information only to:

  • Understand user behavior during the demo phase

Data Safety

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe. However, as this is a demo test phase, data is used only for internal testing purposes.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Any data collected is used only by the RailQuick team for testing.
