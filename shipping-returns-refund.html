Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 2025

This Shipping & Delivery Policy applies to RailQuick during our Test Phase 1 (Demo Version).

Important Notice – Test Phase 1

RailQuick is currently operating in a test/demo phase. You may place test or demo orders on the platform, but no physical delivery to your train seat will take place at this stage.

This phase is only for testing, validation, and improving our service. Full seat delivery will be available very soon after the official launch.

Delivery Policy

During Test Phase 1:

  • Orders placed are for demo and testing purposes only
  • No real-time shipping or seat delivery is guaranteed
  • Delivery timelines shown (if any) are for testing and display only

Returns & Refunds

As this is a demo testing phase:

  • Returns are not applicable
  • Refunds are generally not applicable unless explicitly mentioned
  • Any payment-related issues (if applicable) will be handled manually during testing

Future Launch

Once RailQuick officially launches, a detailed Shipping, Delivery, Returns, and Refund Policy will be updated on this page.
