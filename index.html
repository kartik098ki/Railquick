Kartik Guleria

Founder & CEO

Kartik is a passionate entrepreneur with a vision to revolutionize train travel in India. With a background in technology and a deep understanding of challenges faced by train passengers, he founded RailQuick to solve everyday problems of millions of travelers.

His journey began with a simple observation: train passengers often struggle to access essential items during their journey. This insight led to the creation of RailQuick, India's first on-seat essential delivery service for trains.
